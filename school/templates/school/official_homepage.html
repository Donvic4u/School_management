Who We Are

A school built around excellence and purpose.

Rock Foundation Academy and College is committed to creating an environment where students can learn confidently, discover their potential and develop the values needed to become responsible members of society.

Our approach combines sound academic instruction with discipline, character formation, creativity and leadership.

Academic Excellence Focused teaching and meaningful learning.
Character Values that guide students beyond school.
Leadership Preparing students to make a difference.
Student Growth Supporting the whole learner.

Learning today. Leading tomorrow.

We believe every student has the ability to grow, achieve and contribute meaningfully when given the right environment and guidance.
